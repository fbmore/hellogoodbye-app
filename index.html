Swipe the parts

Subject, hello, message, goodbye, signature — keep a few versions of each and swipe to the one that fits.

Who, and how?

Type an email address and it goes by email. Type a phone number and choose a text message or WhatsApp. Several people at once works too.

Offline? It waits.

Every message is kept in the Outbox first. Queue texts while you’re offline and HelloGoodbye nudges you the moment you’re back.

From any app

Share a photo, PDF or link from any app to HelloGoodbye. It’s attached for you, ready for the same quick “who and how”.

A closer look

Templates that bend

Up to five versions of every part, or as many as you like with Plus. Leave a part blank to skip it. Your choices are remembered for next time.

Email, text or WhatsApp

Hands the finished message to Mail, Messages or WhatsApp — you always see it and tap Send yourself.

Files you always send

Keep a CV, a menu or a map as a canned attachment and include it with a tap. Saved recipient lists, too.

History and people

Everything you send is kept in History, searchable, until you delete it. People builds your address book from who you write to, or import from Contacts.

Follow up, on every device

People and History sync through your private iCloud, so they match on iPhone, iPad and Mac. With Plus, flag a sent message to follow up.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your messages, people and history stay on your devices and in your own private iCloud.
