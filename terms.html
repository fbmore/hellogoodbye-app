1. The service

HelloGoodbye lets you keep message templates, recipient lists, an address book and a history of what you sent, and hands finished messages to Mail, Messages, WhatsApp or another app you choose. It has no ads.

HelloGoodbye is free to download and use. The free version includes templates with up to 5 versions of each part, sending by email, text message and WhatsApp, the share extension, the Outbox and offline queue, the Mac app, saved recipient lists, the address book, iCloud sync of your address book and history, one canned attachment, and the History of the last 30 days.

HelloGoodbye Plus is an optional in-app purchase that adds unlimited versions of each part, unlimited canned attachments, your full History beyond 30 days, follow-up flags on sent messages, and importing and exporting your address book as a spreadsheet (CSV). See section 3.
