HelloGoodbye

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · applies to HelloGoodbye 2.0 and later for iPhone, iPad and Mac

HelloGoodbye helps you write messages faster. Your templates, address book, history and Outbox stay on your device (and, if you are signed in to iCloud, your address book and history also sync through your own private iCloud; templates and the Outbox never do). Messages leave your device only when you send them through Mail, Messages, WhatsApp or another app you choose.

In short  No account. No tracking. No analytics. No ads. We run no servers of our own and never receive your messages, your contacts, your address book or anything else you write in the app. The one outside service is RevenueCat, which handles in-app purchases and sees only an anonymous ID and your App Store purchase records — never your name, email, contacts or messages.

Who we are

HelloGoodbye is made by FRANCESCO BERTOCCI, LLC (“we”). Questions about this policy: fbmore+hellogoodbye@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored on your device, in a private folder shared by the HelloGoodbye app and its share extension (the App Group group.com.fbmore.HelloGoodbye). It is included in your device backups like any other app data, and we cannot access it. If you are signed in to iCloud, your address book and history are also synced through your private iCloud (see iCloud sync); the Outbox and files shared in from other apps are not.

How messages are sent

HelloGoodbye never sends anything by itself. When you tap Send, the finished message is handed to:

  • Mail (or your default email app on the Mac) for email,
  • Messages for text messages,
  • WhatsApp for WhatsApp messages — the WhatsApp app if it is installed, otherwise a wa.me link in your browser, which passes the phone number and message text to WhatsApp,
  • or the app you pick from the share sheet.

You see the message there and confirm it yourself. From that point it is handled by that app and its own privacy policy (for example Apple’s or WhatsApp’s). We never receive a copy.

When a WhatsApp message includes photos or videos, HelloGoodbye copies them to the clipboard and opens the chat with that number and your message, so you can paste them in (other files, like PDFs, go through the share sheet instead and are not copied). On iPhone and iPad the copied files stay on that device only (not Universal Clipboard) and are cleared after 10 minutes; on the Mac they go on the normal clipboard until you copy something else.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your address book (People) and History sync automatically across your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your private iCloud database, using Apple’s CloudKit (container iCloud.com.fbmore.HelloGoodbye). This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy policy; we cannot read it. Templates and the Outbox stay on each device and are not synced, so a message is never sent twice.

Sync is free and on by default. To stop it on a device, turn off “Sync with iCloud” in HelloGoodbye → Settings → iCloud; you can also turn off iCloud for HelloGoodbye in Settings → [your name] → iCloud, or sign out of iCloud. To remove what is already stored there, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

HelloGoodbye Plus is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. To tell the app whether Plus is active, purchases are processed by RevenueCat, which receives:

  • an anonymous app user ID, generated at random by RevenueCat’s software on your device, and
  • your App Store purchase receipt and transaction data (which product, when, and whether it is active, in trial or expired).

No name, email address, contacts, address book, history or message content is sent to RevenueCat. This purchase information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat is the only third-party service HelloGoodbye uses.

Network use

HelloGoodbye has no server of its own. The app makes only these requests itself:

  • a connectivity check: while it believes you are offline, it asks Apple’s standard captive.apple.com page whether the internet is reachable. That request carries no information about you or your messages;
  • RevenueCat, to check and restore purchases (see Purchases);
  • Apple’s iCloud, if you are signed in to iCloud and sync is on (see iCloud sync).

Permissions

  • Contacts: HelloGoodbye does not ask for access to your contacts. When you tap the contact button, Import from Contacts in People, or From Contacts in a saved recipient list (iPhone and iPad), iOS shows its own picker, and only the people, email addresses or phone numbers you choose are given to the app. People you pick are saved in your HelloGoodbye address book.
  • Notifications (optional): used to tell you when you are back online with messages waiting, or that a message shared from another app is ready to finish in HelloGoodbye. Notifications are created on your device; you can turn them off in Settings → Notifications → HelloGoodbye.
  • Files: only the files you pick in Settings or share to HelloGoodbye are read.
  • Background refresh: if messages are waiting for a connection, iOS may briefly wake HelloGoodbye once you are online, to show the back-online notification.

Notifications show who a waiting message is for. With the iOS default “Show Previews: When Unlocked”, that text isn’t visible on the lock screen.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in or email registration
  • No analytics, usage statistics or crash reporting of our own
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location, no access to your whole contacts database, no photos library access
  • No third-party software development kits other than RevenueCat’s, for purchases only
  • No copies of your messages, address book or history on any server of ours

If you choose to share diagnostics with Apple in your device settings, Apple may share crash reports with developers; those are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Contacting us

If you email us (for example with “Send Feedback” in the app), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. We don’t add you to any list.

Exporting and deleting your data

With HelloGoodbye Plus you can export your address book as a spreadsheet (History → People → + → Export Spreadsheet…). The CSV file is created on your device and saved wherever you choose; nothing is sent to us. Importing a spreadsheet works the same way in reverse: the file you pick is read on your device.

Remove templates, lists, attachments and Outbox messages inside the app. In History, swipe a message to delete it; in People, swipe a person to delete them (their messages stay in History until you delete those too). Deleting HelloGoodbye removes everything it stored on that device. If you have used iCloud sync, delete the HelloGoodbye data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Messages you already sent live in Mail, Messages or WhatsApp and are deleted there. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies; if you have a request about them, write to us.

Children

HelloGoodbye is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children; the only data processed outside your devices is the anonymous purchase information described above.
